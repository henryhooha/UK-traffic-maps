# Road Ahead UK — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 4 November 2026_

Road Ahead UK ("the app") is a personal traffic and navigation app. This policy explains what data it uses and why.

## Location
The app uses your device's **precise location**, including while it runs in the background during an active drive (shown by an ongoing "Road Ahead — driving" notification). It uses it to:
- follow your route and warn you about hazards, lane closures, speed restrictions and cameras ahead;
- give turn-by-turn directions;
- place reports you choose to make (e.g. "car stopped") at your position.

Your location history is **not** stored, sold or shared for advertising. When you stop driving, tracking stops.

## Data sent to third-party services
To work, the app sends limited data directly from your device to these services:

| Service | What is sent | Why |
|---|---|---|
| OSRM (routing server, default `router.project-osrm.org`) | Start, via and destination coordinates | Calculating routes |
| Nominatim / OpenStreetMap | The text you search for, and an approximate map area | Finding places |
| Overpass API / OpenStreetMap | Map areas along your route | Speed cameras and junction numbers |
| Transport for London API | Nothing personal | London incidents and roadworks |
| National Highways / other DATEX II feeds (only if you configure them) | Your credentials for that feed | Live motorway signals and incidents |
| OpenStreetMap Notes (only when you tap "Send to OSM") | The camera's location and direction | A public map note for mappers to verify |

Each service has its own privacy policy. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

## Data stored on your device
Your reports, settings and recent destinations are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them. You can delete individual reports in the app. "Export as GPX" creates a file only when you ask for one.

## Notifications and Android Auto
Alerts are shown as notifications and, when connected, in Android Auto. Android Auto may read them aloud. Voice replies are converted to text by Android or Android Auto, then used only to file your report.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Contact
Questions about this policy: contact the developer via the Google Play listing's contact email.
